# The World in One Day — 23 November 2026

Entry for make.fun's "Sum up 23 November's world news in one image" bounty.

- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
- `entry.png` is a 1024x1024 poster drawn by `generate_poster.py` (Python + Pillow):
  COP31's collapse, the Democrats' historic House majority, Russian strikes on the
  Kyiv region and missiles entering Polish airspace, Hormuz shipping attacks at
  their highest level of the war, and Nepal PM Shah's Delhi visit, plus an
  "Also today" strip (Mercury probes, ATP Finals, Libya corvette, Asia's AI
  sell-off, Sudan drone strikes). Headlines cross-checked against SCMP, AFP,
  CNN, Japan Times, WION and Economic Times reporting of 23 November 2026.
